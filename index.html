About StillHaus

A room held between image, memory, and quiet attention.

StillHaus is a digital cultural space shaped for slow looking. It is not built as a portfolio, archive, or spectacle, but as a restrained environment where images can be approached as emotional architecture.

The work unfolds through atmosphere: dim thresholds, careful typography, softened controls, and moments of discovery that ask for patience rather than performance.

Each exhibition is treated as a temporary interior. Visitors move through it as they might through a small museum after closing: gently, privately, and with enough silence for the images to answer.

Current Exhibition

Fallacy & Reflections — Summer 2026

A quiet room for digital dreams.

This first room gathers faces, doubles, mirrors, and fragments of self-recognition. The works look back softly, asking what remains true when identity is seen through ornament, memory, disguise, and light.

Fallacy is not treated here as error, but as a veil: something the mind creates in order to survive beauty, longing, and contradiction. Reflection becomes the threshold where the imagined self and the remembered self briefly stand beside one another.
